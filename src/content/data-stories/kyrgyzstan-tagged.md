I spent a few weeks quietly reading a single Instagram hashtag — **#kyrgyzstan** — and pulling out everything it would give up: who posts under it, where they seem to be from, what language they write in, when the volume spikes, and which posts travel far beyond their author's own followers.

It started as a hunt for foreigners around the World Nomad Games. It didn't stay that way. Most of the accounts had nothing to do with the Games — so this is the honest version: not "who came for the tournament," but **what the tag actually contains**.

A caveat worth stating up front: this is a *sample*, not a census. It was scraped gently and forward-only from the public feed, which is recency-weighted and thins out for older months, so every count here is a lower bound. Origin country isn't a field Instagram exposes for personal accounts — it's inferred from profiles (flag emoji, bio keywords, a linked site's domain), so it undercounts too.
